\section{\textcolor{darkblue}{\textbf{Research Interests}}}
\vspace{-0.4mm}

My research interests lie at the intersection of \textbf{robotics}, \textbf{computer vision}, and \textbf{multimodal learning}, with a particular focus on \textbf{embodied AI}, \textbf{vision-language-action models}, \textbf{world models}, \textbf{robotic manipulation}, and \textbf{open-vocabulary perception}. More broadly, I aim to develop intelligent robotic systems that can \textbf{perceive}, \textbf{reason}, and \textbf{act} with human-like capabilities.
